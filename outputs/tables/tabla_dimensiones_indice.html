Dimensions of the gentrification index
DimensionDescription of the indicator/variable
AgeProportion of the population aged 25-39
OriginProportion of migrants who come from the EU and OECD
Educational levelProportion of the population aged > 25 with higher education
IncomeAverage net income per person
Household compositionProportion of one-person households under the age of 65
Housing marketHousing price (€/m2)
